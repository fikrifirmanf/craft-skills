# Style families

Ten recurring directions, distilled from 152 production systems. Each one is a starting recipe: use a recipe only when it fits the product; adapt it where the content or audience calls for it. Mixing two families works when one dominates (for example, Editorial Warm with a Technical Mono label voice).

Don't choose by what looks cool; choose by what the product needs to communicate.

### 1. Quiet SaaS
**Fits:** B2B products, developer tools with a broad audience, productivity apps.
- Field: near-white with a faint cool cast (#F8FAFC, #FBFBFD); surfaces pure white.
- Ink: 3-step zinc/slate neutrals.
- Accent: one mid-saturation hue (cobalt, indigo, teal) for primary CTA, links and focus. Under 3% of the screen.
- Type: one geometric or neo-grotesk sans; display 48–64px at about −0.03em; optional mono for code snippets.
- Shape: controls 8–10px, cards 12–16px, hairline borders #E5E7EB-ish.
- Signature options: one full-bleed dark band mid-page; a two-tone headline (second line muted); a soft accent wash confined to the hero corners.
- Pitfall: this is the closest family to "generic AI page". It needs a signature move and asymmetric grids to avoid looking templated.

### 2. Dark Console
**Fits:** developer tools, infra, AI, crypto and trading, audio/creative tools.
- Field: near-black (#09090B, #0A0A0A, blue-black #070D18); surfaces step up in lightness (#111, #171717, #1F1F1F).
- Borders: white at 6–10% alpha hairlines; depth from surface steps and hairlines, rarely shadows.
- Accent: one *signal* colour (acid lime, mint, amber, electric blue, cyan), rationed to CTAs, status and active state; sometimes as a small glow.
- Type: tight sans display, mono labels and data; small uppercase micro-labels with positive tracking.
- Effects: faint grid or dot texture (2–4% alpha) behind panels; one radial glow confined to the hero.
- Pitfall: grey-on-black secondary text failing contrast; glows spreading everywhere.

### 3. Editorial Warm
**Fits:** content, publishing, wellness, food, consumer brands, portfolios, premium services.
- Field: paper, cream or linen (#FAF5F1, #F3EFEA, #EEEDEA); ink espresso or warm near-black, not #000.
- Accent: an earthy or saturated warm hue (burnt orange, clay, vermilion, forest), rationed.
- Type: serif display (Didone, Garamond, Playfair-like) with a sans body, *or* a grotesk display with a serif italic accent clause. Generous line-height for reading (1.6–1.7, 60–75 character measure).
- Imagery: full-bleed photography, often warm-toned; hairline rules between sections.
- Shape: low radius or square; little or no shadow.
- Signature options: one inverted near-black band; oversized pull quotes; numbered sections.

### 4. Technical / Blueprint
**Fits:** engineering, hardware, API products, research, architecture.
- Field: off-white drafting paper or deep navy-black "blueprint".
- Hairline grid structure visible in borders and dividers; square corners; monospace annotations, coordinates and index numbers.
- Accent: one high-visibility hue (vermilion, safety orange, magenta, periwinkle).
- Type: grotesk or condensed display, mono for labels; numerals have a strong presence.
- Imagery: line diagrams, node graphs, schematics in place of photos.
- Signature: corner brackets, plus-tick marks at grid intersections, slash-separated labels.

### 5. Monochrome Gallery
**Fits:** portfolios, fashion, architecture, galleries, luxury.
- Achromatic UI chrome; *all* colour comes from content (photos, renders, artwork).
- Huge whitespace; thin or light display weights *or* one enormous wordmark.
- Square edge-to-edge navbar; minimal controls; captions in small caps or mono.
- Pitfall: low-contrast thin grey text. Keep body text at 4.5:1 or above.

### 6. Cinematic Dark
**Fits:** entertainment, games, automotive, launches, events.
- Full-bleed video or photography with dark scrims; near-black field.
- Condensed or extended display type, uppercase micro-labels.
- Accent: one dramatic hue (racing red, ember, gold), sometimes as a gradient confined to the hero's lower third.
- Motion is part of the identity: slow reveals (500–800ms), parallax used sparingly; reduced-motion fallback is required.

### 7. Institutional / Trust
**Fits:** government, finance, healthcare, education, legal, non-profit.
- White or pale field; deep navy, forest or plum brand layer; one clear action colour.
- Readable humanist or neutral sans; conservative scale (display ÷ body ≈ 2–3).
- Square or slightly rounded utility controls; visible focus; plain forms; strong information hierarchy.
- Accessibility is part of the brand here: AA as a minimum, AAA for body text when you can.
- Signature: a solid brand header band, a gold or amber rule marking trust claims, photojournalistic imagery.

### 8. Playful / Illustrated
**Fits:** consumer apps, kids, education, food delivery, community.
- Bright or blush field; 2–4 saturated block colours used as section bands (still with assigned roles).
- Chunky rounded display face; large radius (20–32px); pill buttons.
- Custom illustration or mascot carrying the hero; hand-drawn accents.
- Motion: springy but brief (200–350ms); bounce allowed on small elements only.
- Pitfall: too many colours with no hierarchy. Keep one action colour distinct from the decorative blocks.

### 9. Neo-brutalist
**Fits:** indie products, creative tools, campaigns, youth brands.
- Thick black outlines (2–3px), hard offset shadows (4px 4px 0 #000), flat saturated fills.
- Heavy display weight; often mono or rounded-chunky body.
- Clashing but deliberate palette (2–3 hues plus black and white).
- Pressed state moves the element into its shadow (translate 4px, 4px; shadow 0).
- Pitfall: legibility and tap targets. Keep text contrast and sizes honest despite the style.

### 10. Dense Workbench
**Fits:** admin panels, dashboards, CRMs, IDE-like tools, desktop apps, internal tools.
- Neutral light or dark field with a three-pane shell (sidebar, list, detail) or sidebar plus content.
- Compact density: 13–14px body, 28–36px row heights, 4px base spacing, controls at 6px radius.
- Accent is reserved for selection, focus and primary action; status uses semantic colours paired with icons.
- Type: one UI sans plus tabular numerals; mono for IDs and code.
- Depth from hairlines and tonal surfaces; very little shadow; no decorative gradients.
- Signature: keyboard-first affordances (shortcut hints in mono), command palette, sticky table headers, a precise selection colour.
- The design quality comes from alignment, rhythm and state clarity, not decoration.
