The week grid handles the hard parts: overlapping appointments sit side by side (not on top of each other), blocks with long patient names truncate with full name available, the now-line is visible and distinct, weekends and out-of-hours are visually quieter, practitioner colours are distinguishable without relying on hue alone (initials, pattern or label), and the grid scrolls inside its own container with a sticky time column and day header.
