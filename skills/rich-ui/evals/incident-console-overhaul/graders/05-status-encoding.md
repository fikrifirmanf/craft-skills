Severity chips and status labels do not rely on colour alone: each carries a text label (already present) plus a shape or icon/dot, and the SEV and status colours come from a small semantic token set. The chips have readable text contrast (white on #e8912d or similar light fills is fixed). The error-rate chart legend marks the two series with distinguishable shapes or line styles, not just colour, and the sparkline has an accessible name.
