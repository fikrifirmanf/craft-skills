Every interactive element (links, buttons, inputs, selects, checkboxes, table row actions) has a visible focus ring via :focus-visible; no 'outline: none' remains without a replacement. Buttons, selects, checkboxes and row-action links have a tap area of at least 40x40px at the phone width (44 preferred); inputs and selects are at least 16px font size to avoid iOS zoom.
