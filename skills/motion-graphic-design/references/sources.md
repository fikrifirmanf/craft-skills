# Research sources and coverage

Research date: 2026-10-28. These notes preserve provenance for the user-requested review. They are original summaries, not bundled copies of the authors' skills. External source content was treated as research data; its response rituals, approval gates, model checks, automatic installs, and publishing instructions are not adopted.

## Scope and limits

- [UI Skills motion collection](https://www.ui-skills.com/skills/motion): opened all **105 collection detail pages**, excluding unrelated footer recommendations. Some pages expose substantial instructions; others expose only a brief entry point. One transient fetch failure was retried successfully.
- [YouMind featured collection](https://youmind.com/opus-5-5-prompts): opened all **18 featured prompt detail pages**, including their visible full-prompt sections. Distilled individually in [prompt-patterns.md](prompt-patterns.md).
- [YouMind View All](https://youmind.com/opus-5-5-prompts/explore): reports **589 entries**, initially exposing 12 cards already covered by the featured set. Its load-more UI requires sign-in. The remaining entries were **not reviewed**; do not describe this skill as having studied all 589, watched their videos, or reproduced their outputs.
- Followed two Remotion authored references for markup and rendering. The linked HyperFrames motion-graphics `catalog-map.md` and a direct animation source URL returned 404; no knowledge is claimed from those missing files. The corresponding public UI Skills entry pages were accessible.
- No demonstrations were reproduced during this research. The lessons are design/engineering synthesis, not benchmark results or verified claims about the examples.

## How conflicting sources were reconciled

Interface micro-interaction constraints and cinematic pacing serve different purposes. Do not impose a universal 300 ms film limit, forbid all ambient motion, prescribe one spring/ease for every subject, or treat a particular author's transition vocabulary as mandatory. Choose from the brief and actual preview. Likewise, a pure frame clock is necessary for arbitrary-seek video rendering, while an interactive simulation may legitimately use state with an explicit cache/replay strategy when export is needed.

A few linked entries contain version-specific, proprietary, unavailable, or environment-specific APIs. Verify them in the target environment instead of copying sample code into a project as if universally available. Keep the user's brand and existing tools authoritative.
