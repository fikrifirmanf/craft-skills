# Prompt patterns from the studied examples

These are original distilled patterns, not copied prompts. The 18 publicly featured detail pages were opened on 2026-10-28. Their source links appear below. Some entries are tutorials or descriptions of results, rather than complete executable briefs. Treat the model labels and engagement counts as catalog metadata, not proof of quality or capability.

## Turn an idea into a production brief

Use the relevant fields, without making the user fill in a long questionnaire:

> Create [deliverable] for [audience], conveying [one central message]. Use [duration, dimensions, fps, delivery format]. The visual language is [specific typography, palette, geometry, materials, light, framing]. The sequence is [timed actions with subject and camera intent]. Synchronize [events] to [word times/music cues] when sound is included. Keep [copy, parameters, assets] editable. Respect [asset restrictions]. Implement a reproducible timeline with seek/reset. Inspect [critical frames and handoffs], watch a draft, then produce and verify [requested output].

For interactive work, add explicit controls, legal states, interrupted transitions, direct stage selection, and reset. For an effect, define what it does to the message and how it resolves. A precise particle count or fashionable engine is less useful than a clear visible behavior and measured performance target.

## Example adaptations

**Type-led product reveal:** A 12-second 4:5 sequence with two short claims, one real demonstration, and the logo. A masked word cascade introduces the first claim; the final word becomes the demonstration's frame; a result becomes the end mark. Derive styling from the product. Keep the complete meaning without sound and verify the encoded MP4.

**Mechanism lesson:** An interactive cutaway with exterior, opening, operation, and exploded/reassembled states. Build named parts with rest transforms, choose cameras for visibility, allow direct selection and reset, and export a fixed camera/timing pass if a video is requested.

**Rewindable abstract loop:** A fixed seeded field resolves into an original mark and disperses along analytic paths. Expose time in the preview, sample the same state for rendering, and inspect both visual and velocity continuity at the loop boundary.

These examples are directions to instantiate with the user's subject, not a mandatory menu to present before doing work.
